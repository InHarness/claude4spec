The two records disagree about the parity they both claim. Built: `createPage` (services/page-write.ts) returns the settled `content`; the `create_page` tool answers it; `POST /api/pages/:rootId` strips it and keeps the five-field DTO. Needed from the spec: either add `content` to `create-page-response` (and drop its echo-free sentence), or record that the REST answer deliberately differs from the tool's and drop the parity sentence.
