## What I found

`ux690pt2` says that under another effective address the token is neither sent nor deleted and that returning to the issuer restores the session, while `remote-session` keeps the table single-row (login = upsert, logout = delete, a 401 deletes the session). The pages do not say which session a logout or a 401 deletes when the stored row was issued under another address, nor whether a login under the other address may replace it. Assumed: logout and the 401 wipe delete only the row issued under the CURRENT effective address (a session issued elsewhere is not this project's login here and stays for its issuer); a successful login under another address replaces the single row as before (single-row upsert), so the return-to-issuer restore holds only while no login happened elsewhere.

## Suggestion

State it in the specification. Implemented meanwhile as: ASSUMPTION:dev-0801 — logout and the 401 wipe delete only the session of the current effective address; a login elsewhere replaces the single row.
