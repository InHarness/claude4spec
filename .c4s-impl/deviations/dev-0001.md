## What I found

A root at `.` would then observe node_modules, dist, build, coverage, out — before 2.1.60 every mount ignored these heavy directories. The implementation keeps excluding them for registry roots and for M33 plugin sources (on top of the dot-segment rule), so a root at `.` does not observe node_modules. Please confirm, or state in M02/M40 that the heavy build/dependency directories are outside a root's observed subtree.
