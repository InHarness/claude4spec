## What I found

Section `z7l89jhf` of `pages/modules/m13-entity-framework/L2-domain.md` ("Plugin contract — finalna anatomia", ~45 KB at 2.1.63) is larger than one `c4s release-diff` response, even when read alone with `--section-limit 1`. ## Suggestion

Split the section into sub-sections (e.g. one per slot of the contract: card / chip / row / overlay / prompt blocks), so each one fits a single release-diff read. Another option is to let `release-diff` page within a section (a byte or line range).
