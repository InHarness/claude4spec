## What I found

M19 m19l12sc (2.1.16) names the list-page pattern as `EntityListHeader` with search → `TagFilterBar` in the header's `filters` slot. The kit's `TagFilterBar` is a full-width, self-bordered strip (own `px-8` padding, bottom border, 'Filter by tag:' label, chips wrapping) — it does not fit an inline flex slot between the 280px search box and the actions. Plugin list pages (database-tables, mcp-tools) stack it as a sibling row below the header inside `EntityListLayout.header`.

## Suggestion

Assumed in code: keep that stacking; the pattern's intent (filter between search and the list, all from /ui) holds. either a compact `TagFilterBar` variant for the slot, or the pattern text saying `TagFilterBar` sits under the header in `EntityListLayout.header`.
