## What I found

The spec-operation record `release-restore` (2.1.26) is addressed by `releaseName` alone and lists one code, RELEASE_NOT_FOUND. Its only rendering, `POST /api/releases/:name/restore`, also takes a body `scope` (`spec` default, `entity`, `page`) with a `target`, and answers 400 VALIDATION when `scope: entity` lacks `target.type`/`target.slug` or `scope: page` lacks `target.path`. Built (implementer): the catalog row declares the record as written — input `releaseName`, codes [RELEASE_NOT_FOUND]; the route is unchanged. Needed from the spec: either record the partial-restore scopes (addressing and VALIDATION in `kodyBledow`) or state that the operation is the whole-spec restore and the partial scopes belong to another operation (or are to be removed).
