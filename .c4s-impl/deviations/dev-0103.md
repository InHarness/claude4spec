## What I found

M31 lists the refusals of an invalid `<home>/.claude4spec/workspace.json` (WORKSPACE_FILE_INVALID / _PROJECT_ID_MISSING / _PROJECT_ID_DUPLICATE) as refusals of the START of that workspace, but says nothing about what every other reader of the registry sees while one home's file is broken: `c4s list-workspaces`, GET /api/workspaces of a server running for another workspace, `c4s` project resolution across workspaces, and registration in another workspace.

## Suggestion

State it in the specification. Implemented meanwhile as: only the start of the owning workspace (and writes into that file) refuse. Every other read keeps answering — the broken entry is listed with its registry attributes, no projects and no plugins (`projectCount: 0` in `c4s list-workspaces` and GET /api/workspaces), and carries the refusal (code + message naming the file); `c4s list-workspaces` prints the refusal on stderr while its rows stay complete; a project-id miss in a resolution covering the broken workspace appends the refusal to its PROJECT_ID_NOT_FOUND message.
